## Authority order

1. The approved WSQ Course Proposal v2 and TSC Mapping define the fixed course facts: Problem Management ICT-OUS-4011-1.1, three learning outcomes, K1–K5, A1–A6, 14 delivery hours, and 60-minute WA plus 60-minute Case Study.
2. The Drive-held v2 candidate papers define the controlled assessment structure: five WA questions and three Case Study tasks. Instrument, count, timing and K/A mapping are preserved.
3. The legacy v3 PowerPoint is instructional evidence only. Useful eight-domain coverage was retained; the legacy visual design and erroneous cloud-solution TSC slide were not reused.
4. The DevOps Institute v3.4 Study Guide supplies the eight-domain certification blueprint and vocabulary.
5. Current PeopleCert and DORA sources provide time-sensitive context. The course distinguishes the v3.4 guide's historical four-key wording from DORA's current five-metric model.
