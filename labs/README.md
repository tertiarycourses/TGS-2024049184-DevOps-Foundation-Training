# DevOps Foundation Training — R Labs

Course code: TGS-2024049184
Version: v4.0

Eight connected, self-contained labs turn DevOps problem-management concepts into reproducible evidence. Follow them in order; Lab 08 is the capstone.

## [Lab 01 — Build an Incident-to-Problem Lifecycle Baseline in R](Lab-01-incident-lifecycle-baseline/README.md)

Apply K1 and A1 by managing a supplied incident dataset through detection, logging, investigation, resolution and learning states.

## [Lab 02 — Detect Actionable Service Signals in R](Lab-02-automated-signal-detection/README.md)

Apply K2 and A2 by designing an automated detection rule that produces specific, severity-ranked problem signals without excessive noise.

## [Lab 03 — Investigate Recurring Failure Causes with Pareto Analysis](Lab-03-root-cause-pareto/README.md)

Apply K2 and A2 while preparing evidence for A3: distinguish symptoms from cause candidates and identify recurring categories worth deeper investigation.

## [Lab 04 — Prioritise Problems by Severity, Frequency and Impact](Lab-04-problem-prioritisation/README.md)

Apply K3 and A3 by defining evidence, then K4 and A4 by applying a transparent prioritisation rule.

## [Lab 05 — Diagnose Pipeline Flow and Cultural Debt](Lab-05-pipeline-risk-review/README.md)

Apply K4 and A4 by combining stage performance with visible queue and rework evidence to recommend a prioritised improvement.

## [Lab 06 — Balance Reliability, Security and Delivery Guardrails](Lab-06-reliability-security-guardrails/README.md)

Apply K4 and A4 by categorising reliability and security risks, then selecting proportional automated guardrails.

## [Lab 07 — Build a DORA and Problem-Management Evidence Dashboard](Lab-07-dora-problem-dashboard/README.md)

Apply K5 and A6 by calculating documented delivery metrics and linking them to tracked problem-management actions.

## [Lab 08 — Capstone — Produce a Verified DevOps Problem Recommendation](Lab-08-capstone-problem-record/README.md)

Apply A1–A6 in one connected case: manage the lifecycle, analyse root cause, prioritise, recommend controls and create monitorable documentation.
